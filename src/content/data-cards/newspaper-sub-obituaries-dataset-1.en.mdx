# Welsh Language Obituaries

## About the data

* **What?** - 15,836 Welsh language obituaries extracted from Welsh Newspapers Online. These span 1804-1919 and provide details of the lives of notable, and ordinary people from all over Wales and beyond. OCR data is raw and does contain errors.
* **Who?** - This data forms part of the larger Welsh Newspapers Online dataset created and managed by the National Library of Wales&#x20;
