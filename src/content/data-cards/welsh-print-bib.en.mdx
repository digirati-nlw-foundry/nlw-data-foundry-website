# Title: Bibliography of Welsh Printed Material

## About the data

* **What?** - Welcome to the ‘beta’ version of the Bibliography of Welsh Printed Materials: a list of physical books recently acquired and catalogued by the National Library. This is a first step in creating a fuller bibliography of Welsh and Celtic Books published in recent years that are held here in the library. This initial version comprises a spreadsheet of bibliographic data: titles, names of author(s), publication information, subject(s), and material manifestation e.g. details of page numbers and illustrations. 
* **Who?** - This initial version comprises a spreadsheet of bibliographic data: titles, names of author(s), publication information, subject(s), and material manifestation e.g. details of page numbers and illustrations, created by staff.

## About the data source

### Description

All the materials relate to Wales in some way – whether they be written by a Welsh author, published by a Wales-based publisher, or about Wales or Welsh life. The majority of titles have been acquired by the library through its status as a Legal Deposit Library, which gives it a right to one copy of each book published in the United Kingdom.
