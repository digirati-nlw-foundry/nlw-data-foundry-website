# Aberystwyth Ship Masters Data

## About the data

* **What?** - Collection of biographical data for captains of historical ships registered to the Port of Aberystwyth. The data includes date and place of birth and death, occupations and other personal information as free text, along with the names and registration numbers of ships they were asssciated with, connecting this data with Aberystwyth Shipping Records Data.
* **Who?** - This dataset was researched and collected by volunteers using original genealogical records and records held at The National Library of Wales as part of the NLW Volunteering Programme.
