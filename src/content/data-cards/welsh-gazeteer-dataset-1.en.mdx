# Open Data Gazetteer of Welsh Place Names

## About the data

* **What?** - A Welsh/English Gazetteer of Welsh placenames, including settlements, buildings, geographic features and archeological sites. The gazetteer includes alternative forms in Welsh and English (Hash seperated) and coordinates. It also inlcudes ID's form the SNARC and Wikidata entities for each place. Where standerdised spellings have been agreed by the Welsh Language Commisison or Welsh goverment, the approved form is used.
* **Who?** - This dataset is the result of long term collaboration and data sharing with the Welsh Language Commission, Welsh Government as well as crowd sourcing activities.
