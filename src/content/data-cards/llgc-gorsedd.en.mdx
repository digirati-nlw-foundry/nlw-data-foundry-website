# Title: Gorsedd Cymru

## About the data

* **What?** - Gorsedd is a comprehensive database of individuals who have been admitted to the Gorsedd at the National Eisteddfod of Wales. Gorsedd is a 'Beta' website for researchers, students and members of the public interested in the history of the Eisteddfod and Welsh culture.
* **Who?** - This dataset was compiled from items in the National Library of Wales collections and recorded by volunteers as part of the National Library of Wales Volunteering Programme. We would like to extend our sincere thanks to our volunteers, Nonna, Christine, Ian, Martine and especially Owen Watkin for their dedication and valuable contributions to this project.

## About the data source

### Description

The website contains information about bardic names, locations, type of membership, and their contribution to Welsh culture. The data has been collected from reliable historical sources by volunteers at the National Library of Wales.
Note: Not all profiles are complete, it depends on the information available to the volunteers.

### References to abbreviations of the sources of information about the Members of the Order

* **HGB:** History of the Gorsedd of Bards, Geraint and Zonia Bowen (Llansannan, Denbigh, 1991).
* **LMG (List of Members of the Gorsedd):** 1947, 1959, 1969.
* **NLW MSS (National Library of Wales Manuscripts):** Manuscripts and papers of the National Library of Wales.
* **Bangor MSS:** Manuscripts and papers of Bangor University.
* **Comp.:** Companion to Welsh Literature (Cardiff, University of Wales Press, 1997).
